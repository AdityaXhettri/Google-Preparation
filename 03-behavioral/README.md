# Behavioral Stories Bank (Googleyness & Hiring Manager)

> **IMPORTANT:** These must be *your* real stories. No copying. Interviewers ask deep follow-ups — fake stories fall apart in 2 questions.
>
> Use **STAR**: Situation → Task → Action → Result (with metrics)

## Story Buckets — pick your strongest 1-2 per category

### 1. Technical Leadership
- Drove a technical decision that moved a project forward
- Led design/code review or architecture discussion
- Mentored a junior engineer

### 2. Conflict / Disagreement
- Disagreed with a peer/manager — how resolved
- Handled a difficult stakeholder
- Pushed back on a bad technical decision

### 3. Ambiguity
- Took a vague problem and defined it yourself
- Built something with minimal direction
- Handled shifting priorities mid-project

### 4. Failure / Learning
- A project that failed — what you learned
- A bug you caused in production
- A decision you'd make differently

### 5. Impact / Scale
- A project that measurably moved a metric (revenue, perf, users)
- Optimized something by X%
- Shipped something users actually loved

### 6. Collaboration / Inclusion
- Worked across teams/teams/teams
- Made someone else successful
- Improved team process (CI, on-call, docs)

## Googlyness — Google's 10 Things to Look For
- Humility, ownership, bias for action, curiosity, efficient communication, courage, persistence, impact, collaboration, customer focus

## Question I should be ready for
- "Tell me about a time you had to influence without authority"
- "Describe a situation where you disagreed with your manager"
- "Tell me about the most technically challenging project you've worked on"
- "How do you handle ambiguity?"
- "What's a risk you took and what was the outcome?"

## Practice routine
- 1 mock answer per week (record yourself or pair with friend)
- Keep answers ~2 minutes (concise STAR)
- Always end with: **what I learned / what I'd do differently**
