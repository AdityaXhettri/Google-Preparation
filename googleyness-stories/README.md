## Practice routine

1. Pick 1 question
2. Pick 1 story
3. Tell it in 90-120 seconds, out loud
4. Record yourself (phone voice memo) — listen back
5. Identify weak spots: too long, missing metrics, no clear action YOU took
6. Re-tell, shorter

**Target:** each of my 6 stories rehearsed at least 5 times, can deliver in <2 min, can answer 3-4 follow-ups each.

## DON'T DO

- ❌ Copy stories from others' repos or interviews
- ❌ Use stories you weren't the primary owner of
- ❌ Fabricate metrics
- ❌ Use stories >3 years old (interviewer will ask why you didn't have one recently)

## DO

- ✅ Use projects from `../projects/` once built
- ✅ Include actual numbers (improved X by Y%, served Z users)
- ✅ Highlight YOUR specific decisions/actions, not the team's
- ✅ End with what you LEARNED (growth signal)
