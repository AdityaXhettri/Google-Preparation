## Story (STAR format, ~2 min spoken)

### Situation
_What was the context? Who was involved? What was unclear?_

### Task
_What was YOUR specific responsibility? What did you need to figure out?_

### Action
_3-5 bullet points. What did YOU do? Be specific. Include:_
- _How you gathered info (asked who, read what)_
- _How you proposed options (presented to whom)_
- _What trade-offs you weighed_
- _How you decided_

### Result
_What happened? Include metrics:_
- _Shipped in X weeks_
- _Used by Y people / Z requests/day_
- _Saved $X / improved latency by Y%_
- _What you'd do differently_

## Follow-up questions I should be ready for

1. "Why did you choose option A over option B?"
2. "What would have happened if you made the wrong call?"
3. "How did you know when you were done / when to stop exploring?"
4. "Did anyone disagree with your approach?"
5. "What was the hardest part?"

_Open with the result, then explain how you handled the ambiguity._
